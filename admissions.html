Admission information

For accurate admission details, please contact the school directly. The following checklist is a general guide and must be confirmed by the school.

Documents to ask the school about
  • Student birth certificate
  • Previous school records, if applicable
  • Parent/guardian contact information
  • Passport-size photographs, if required
  • Any additional documents specified by the school
Admission process
  1. Submit an enquiry.
  2. Speak with the school office.
  3. Confirm eligibility and document requirements.
  4. Follow the school's official application process.
